# Approved direction — organic tech

**Approved by:** Rich, 2026-09-03, in session.
**Chosen comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/a.html` — variant **A, "the home is the nature"**.
Rendered comparison set: `a.html` (chosen), `b.html` (literal botanical, rejected),
`c.html` (organic form only, rejected).

## The pinned world
Rich supplied four reference frames from an "organic tech" design deck and asked
for that look across the website and, later, the Flutter app. The frames are kept
at `.impeccable/references/organic-tech-1.jpg` … `-4.jpg`. Their signature moves:
real photography of living things, dark glass pill controls floating on it, one
acid-lime signal colour, circular rings as the data form, clean geometric grotesk,
pale mint grounds.

Because the world was brief-pinned by the user, no concept roll was run
(`concept-seed.mjs` not invoked). A user- or brief-pinned direction beats the roll.

## Rich's three answers
1. **Nature literalness — A, "the home is the nature."** WavePresence's own product
   photography carries the organic half; glass pills and lime rings carry the tech
   half. Rejected: literal botanical (reads as a plant-care app), and organic form
   only (true to the mechanism but cold for an eldercare buyer).
2. **Ground — light, with dark glass moments.** Not the dark forest option. The
   buyer is 50–70, on a phone, usually in daylight, deciding whether to purchase.
3. **Scope — both pages.** `/start` and the landing page. The landing page was
   stale: it still sold a wristband and a phone/watch tracking model that the
   product no longer has.

## What carries forward to the app
The same tokens and grammar in `assets/organic.css`: pale mint ground, moss slabs,
one lime signal for live/chosen, Manrope 800 at tight tracking, large radii, glass
over photography, rings for time-of-day data. The room-activity dial on the landing
page is the data language the app's dashboard should inherit.
