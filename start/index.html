We don’t watch people. We watch rooms.

People deserve to be looked after without being watched. WavePresence never uses a camera or a microphone. It uses radar, pressure, door, light and humidity sensors, and each one only sees what it needs to.

  • Nothing to wear. No wristband, no tag, no phone in a pocket.

Bedroom sensors

Each one holds several sensors: breathing and heart micro-motion, body motion, and heat. With the floor mat and door sensor, that combination is what gives families peace of mind. Nothing is worn.

Living-space sensors

Presence and movement only. No breathing or heart reading, and none needed. They tell you which room is in use when the bedroom is empty.

Fall sensor

Feels the floor itself. One per floor senses a fall anywhere on it, in any room. Working name; we may rename it.
